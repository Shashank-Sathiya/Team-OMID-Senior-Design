## 2. Vision and Objective

Team OMID is dedicated to engineering transparent organic photovoltaic (OPV) modules that harvest non-visible light (ultraviolet and infrared) for agricultural energy generation. Our solution prevents the obstruction of visible light necessary for crop photosynthesis, avoiding the environmental and spatial limitations of traditional opaque silicon solar panels.

## 3. Expectations and Ground Rules

### 3a. Meetings & Communication

* **Primary Communication Channel:** Discord
* **Team Syncs:** Weekly internal meetings to review task progress, resolve blockers, and prepare deliverables.
* **Advisor Meetings:** Formal weekly check-ins with Dr. Mohsen Rezayat to validate scope, review technical direction, and secure guidance.

### 3b. Decision-Making & Conflict Resolution

* **Consensus-Driven Decisions:** Major architectural, modeling, and tool-selection decisions will be made via group consensus during weekly syncs.
* **Deadlock Resolution:** If consensus cannot be reached within 48 hours, the team will defer to the respective pipeline or domain lead, or escalate the decision to our faculty advisor, Dr. Rezayat.
* **Accountability:** Members are expected to communicate potential delays at least 24 hours prior to internal deadlines to allow the team to redistribute workloads effectively.
